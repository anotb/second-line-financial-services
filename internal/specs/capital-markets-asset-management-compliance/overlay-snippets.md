Per the strict sector-overlay rule, capability skills do not get duplicated under sector plugins. Where the capital-markets overlay adds material nuance to a capability skill, the nuance lives as a sector-overlay reference doc inside the capability skill, owned by the capability plugin's spec sheet. This file lists the capital-markets overlay needs the capital-markets sector lead identifies for each capability skill.

## consumer-compliance-fair-lending — applicability assessment

Generally not applicable. The skills under consumer-compliance-fair-lending (UDAAP review, adverse-action review, marketing-claim review, complaint-theme analysis, Section 1071 readiness, fair-servicing review) target depository and consumer-credit lending activity. Investment advisers, broker-dealers, and asset managers do not extend consumer credit and are not subject to ECOA/Reg B/Reg V/CFPB UDAAP authority for advisory services.

Narrow cross-applicability cases the audit memo should flag:
- BDs that engage in margin lending under Reg T are not consumer credit for ECOA, but margin disclosure and customer protection are not nothing. They sit in Reg BI / FINRA suitability lanes, not in consumer-compliance-fair-lending.
- BDs that distribute branded credit cards or affiliated-bank deposit accounts can incur consumer-compliance reach via the bank affiliate. That is the bank affiliate's consumer-compliance program, not the BD's. The BD's role is conflict disclosure and best-interest analysis under Reg BI.
- BD/IA marketing under the Marketing Rule overlaps in spirit with UDAAP "fair and balanced" reasoning. The reach is doctrinally distinct (antifraud under §206 vs. UDAAP under Dodd-Frank §1031). Marketing Rule is the operative lane (see marketing-rule-evidence in this plugin).

Phase 2 decision: no capital-markets overlay file under any consumer-compliance-fair-lending skill. The cross-applicability cases above are documented here in the audit memo and not propagated as overlay docs.
