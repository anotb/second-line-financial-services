# FFIEC CDD high-risk customer review

## Source anchors

- FFIEC BSA/AML CDD manual states risk-based CDD helps banks understand the nature and purpose of customer relationships, develop customer risk profiles, conduct ongoing monitoring, and identify suspicious activity.
- 31 CFR 1010.230 requires covered financial institutions to identify and verify beneficial owners of legal entity customers when a new account is opened, unless an exclusion or exemption applies.

## Scenario

A legal entity customer has a complex ownership structure, higher-risk geography exposure, expected international wires, and a recent unexplained increase in transaction volume.

## Review focus

| Area | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Customer risk profile | Initial risk rating, rationale, risk factors, approval |
| Beneficial ownership | Certification or equivalent collection record, verification evidence, exemption rationale if applicable |
| Nature and purpose | Business description, expected activity, source of funds or source of wealth, geography, products |
| Ongoing monitoring | Alert history, activity trend, refresh triggers, customer information updates |
| EDD escalation | Additional information collected, reviewer notes, escalation decision |

## Human-review gate

The skill or agent may draft an EDD packet but must not decide to maintain, exit, file, or not file. Compliance officer review required.
