# Interagency third-party risk contract gap review

## Source anchors

- U.S. interagency third-party risk guidance covers planning, due diligence and selection, contract negotiation, ongoing monitoring, and termination.
- The guidance states that third-party use does not remove a banking organization's responsibility to operate safely and soundly and comply with applicable laws and regulations.

## Scenario

A banking organization reviews a contract for a fintech platform supporting account opening and transaction notifications. The contract includes service descriptions and security language but has incomplete audit, subcontractor, incident notification, data ownership, and termination provisions.

## Gap table

| Lifecycle area | Draft gap | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Due diligence | Security and financial-condition evidence not indexed to approval decision | Due diligence checklist, risk acceptance, approval memo |
| Contract | Subcontractor notification and approval rights unclear | Contract clause, subcontractor schedule, change notification process |
| Ongoing monitoring | Monitoring frequency and reporting obligations not defined | SLA report, incident report, periodic review procedure |
| Termination | Exit support and data return or destruction terms incomplete | Exit plan, data destruction certificate template |

## Human-review gate

Legal, third-party risk, information security, business owner, and compliance review required before onboarding or renewal.
