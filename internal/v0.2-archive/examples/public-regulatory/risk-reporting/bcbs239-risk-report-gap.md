# BCBS 239 risk report gap assessment

## Source anchors

- BCBS 239 was issued after the financial crisis highlighted that many banks could not aggregate risk exposures and concentrations fully, quickly, and accurately.
- The principles focus on risk data aggregation capabilities and internal risk reporting practices.

## Scenario

A risk committee pack includes credit, liquidity, operational, cyber, third-party, and AI risk metrics. Metric owners use different definitions for breach status and manually adjust several values without documented lineage.

## Gap analysis

| Principle theme | Draft issue | Control improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Governance | Metric ownership and approval of definitions not consistently documented | Maintain KRI dictionary with owner approval and change history |
| Accuracy and integrity | Manual adjustments not traceable to source records | Require adjustment log, evidence, and reviewer sign-off |
| Completeness | Report does not show missing-source indicators | Add completeness control and exception queue |
| Timeliness | Late source feeds not escalated before committee publication | Add timeliness SLA, breach escalation, and report freeze procedure |
| Adaptability | New AI and third-party concentration metrics added outside change process | Require metric onboarding workflow and committee approval |

## Human-review gate

Risk reporting owner should validate metric definitions, data lineage, and committee escalation before publication.
