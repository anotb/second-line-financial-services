# Cloud exit plan for critical ICT service

## Source anchors

- EBA DORA materials require comprehensive ICT third-party arrangement information and support supervisory assessment of concentration and critical ICT third-party providers.
- U.S. interagency third-party guidance includes termination as a lifecycle stage.

## Minimum exit-plan content

1. Critical service and supported business function.
2. Trigger events for exit or transition.
3. Substitute service or internal fallback options.
4. Data extraction, validation, transfer, retention, and destruction steps.
5. Identity, access, encryption, logging, and monitoring changes.
6. Operational timeline, roles, dependencies, and decision gates.
7. Last test date, results, issues, and remediation.

## Evidence expected

| Evidence | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Exit strategy | Shows planned transition path |
| Data inventory | Defines scope of transferable data |
| Test result | Demonstrates feasibility |
| Contract clause map | Confirms provider obligations |
| Concentration analysis | Supports resilience and substitutability assessment |
