# SEC cyber disclosure readiness

## Source anchors

- SEC cybersecurity disclosure guidance says domestic registrants must file material cybersecurity incident disclosure on Form 8-K within four business days after determining that the incident is material.
- The timing is tied to the materiality determination, not discovery, and materiality should be determined without unreasonable delay.

## Scenario

A public financial institution identifies unauthorized access to a customer-facing application. The incident response team is still investigating scope, affected systems, customer impact, business interruption, and financial impact.

## Readiness checklist

| Area | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Incident chronology | Detection time, escalation time, containment actions, current status |
| Materiality workflow | Disclosure committee involvement, legal review, finance input, investor-impact analysis |
| Technical sensitivity | Review whether proposed disclosure avoids sensitive technical details that could impede response |
| Governance | Board or committee notification record and management oversight evidence |
| Update process | Criteria for amendment or further disclosure as facts evolve |

## Human-review gate

Securities counsel and disclosure committee must make final materiality and filing decisions.
