# Public regulatory scenarios

These scenarios are not tied to named institutions. They are testable examples built from public regulatory sources so the repo can be used without confidential client or firm data.

| Scenario | Primary sources | Related skill |
|---|---|---|
| AI creditworthiness triage | EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OCC 2026 MRM guidance | `ai-act-triage` |
| Agentic AI control design | NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OCC 2026 MRM guidance | `agentic-ai-controls` |
| ICT third-party register | DORA register of information materials, EBA DORA oversight materials | `dora-register-builder` |
| Third-party lifecycle review | U.S. interagency third-party risk management guidance | `contract-gap-review` |
| BCBS 239 reporting gap | BCBS 239 | `bcbs239-gap-assessment` |
| Cyber disclosure readiness | SEC cybersecurity disclosure guide | `cyber-disclosure-readiness` |
| Section 1071 readiness | CFPB Section 1071 and Regulation B section 1002.107 | `section1071-readiness` |
| CDD ongoing monitoring | FFIEC BSA/AML CDD manual and beneficial ownership rule | `cdd-risk-review` |
