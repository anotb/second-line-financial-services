# Source anchors — qa-workpaper

The named, dated audit-quality and supervisory sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

## Primary frame — audit quality, supervision, and documentation review

- **AICPA AU-C 220, Quality Management for an Engagement Conducted in Accordance With Generally Accepted Auditing Standards** (Statements on Auditing Standards, AICPA Professional Standards, current edition; supersedes prior AU-C 220 effective for periods beginning on or after December 15, 2025) [verify paragraph references]. Engagement-level quality framework: engagement partner responsibility for quality, direction, supervision, and review of the engagement; engagement quality review where required; documentation of the EQR; resolution of differences of opinion. The audit-quality methodology anchor that second-line testing programs borrow when framing QA over a completed workpaper. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 220").

- **AICPA AU-C 230, Audit Documentation** (Statements on Auditing Standards). The experienced-auditor sufficiency standard: documentation should be sufficient for an experienced auditor with no prior connection to the engagement to understand the nature, timing, extent, and results of audit procedures performed; the audit evidence obtained; the conclusions reached on significant matters; and significant judgments [verify paragraph references against current AICPA codification, generally AU-C 230 §08-§14]. The QA reviewer reads the workpaper against this bar. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 230").

- **AICPA AU-C 265, Communicating Internal Control Related Matters Identified in an Audit**. Severity hierarchy distinguishing deficiency, significant deficiency, and material weakness [verify paragraph references]. Used by the QA reviewer to challenge severity calibration on the workpaper's exception classification and conclusion language. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 265").

- **AICPA AU-C 530, Audit Sampling**, especially §A18-§A22 on evaluating sample results [verify paragraph references against current AICPA codification]. The QA reviewer's frame for re-perform of selected sample items and for challenging projection-from-sample-to-population reads in the workpaper's aggregation section. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 530").

- **IIA Global Internal Audit Standards — Standard 1300 (Quality Assurance and Improvement Program)** and **Standard 2340 (Engagement Supervision)** [verify Standard numbers and section labels against the IIA's Global Internal Audit Standards effective January 9, 2025; prior IPPF Performance Standards 1300 and 2340 map to the new Standards' QAIP and engagement-supervision requirements]. The QAIP anchor for periodic and ongoing internal-quality assessments; supervision-review expectation that engagement work is reviewed before issuance. https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/ .

- **IIA Practice Guide on Quality Assurance and Improvement Program** (current edition) [verify edition year and section labels]. Operational guidance on internal QA assessment design, engagement-level QA, and external assessment cadence. Used by second-line testing functions that operate under the Global Internal Audit Standards or borrow IIA methodology. https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/ .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Audit booklet (April 2012)**, §IV (audit testing) and §V (audit communication and reporting) [verify section labels against the current edition]. Examiner-facing detail on internal audit and IT audit documentation review and communication expectations at federally-supervised banks. Used as the bank-supervisory anchor for QA over testing-program documentation. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/audit/ .

- **SR 08-8 — Compliance Risk Management Programs and Oversight at Large Banking Organizations with Complex Compliance Profiles** (Federal Reserve, October 16, 2008) [verify section labels for the testing-program independent-review expectations]. Federal Reserve supervisory expectation that compliance-testing programs carry independent review of testing work product. https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2008/sr0808.htm .

- **OCC PPM 5310-3 — Bank Supervision Process** [verify exact PPM number, section labels, and current effective date]. OCC examiner-facing supervision-process guidance referenced by OCC-supervised bank compliance and audit programs as the testing-documentation and independent-review anchor [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html [verify URL].

- **COSO 2013 Internal Control — Integrated Framework, Principle 17** (the entity evaluates and communicates internal-control deficiencies in a timely manner to those parties responsible for taking corrective action) [verify principle reference]. Used as the framework anchor for QA challenging whether the workpaper's exception elevation and conclusion adequately communicate deficiencies. https://www.coso.org/internal-control .

- **PCAOB QC 1000, A Firm's System of Quality Control** (PCAOB Release No. 2024-005, May 13, 2024) [verify section labels and the current-effective-date posture; the rule was approved by the SEC in August 2024 with a phased effective date]. Borrowed as a methodology reference for engagement-level monitoring and remediation expectations; second-line testing functions are not subject to PCAOB QC standards directly but may borrow the framework when the engagement is supporting external audit reliance scoping. https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/quality-control-standards .

## Bank-specific anchors (banking overlay carries the detail)

- **CFPB Compliance Management Review** examination procedures, embedded in the **CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify section labels]. Standing examination procedures for the CMS, including the testing-program independent-review component; the CMS-finding pattern when independent QA over testing-program work is absent or weak. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/ .

- **OCC Comptroller's Handbook — Compliance Management Systems** [verify current edition and section labels]. OCC examiner-facing CMS review including independent-quality-assurance expectations for testing programs. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D (OCC Heightened Standards)** for $50B+ covered banks; testing-program QA outputs feed the risk-governance framework and the board's third-line oversight, including the independent-risk-management standards on testing rigor [verify subsection labels]. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30 .

- **FFIEC Consumer Compliance Rating System** (Federal Register, 80 Fed. Reg. 67428, November 2, 2015) [verify Federal Register citation and section]. Frames the consumer-compliance rating that examiner findings (often built on second-line testing reviewed via QA) feed [verify section]. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2015/11/02/2015-27738/ffiec-consumer-compliance-rating-system [verify URL].

- **SEC Division of Examinations risk alerts on compliance-program documentation** (current alerts; refresh by year) [verify alert IDs and dates]. ## Cross-cutting cyber anchors (cyber overlay carries the detail)

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500.7 — Access Privileges and Management** [verify subsection labels against the latest amendment]. The access-recertification workpaper is a frequent QA target; vendor-supplied scan results and screenshot-only access reviews are recurring evidence-reliability QA findings. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500.17 — Notices to Superintendent**, §500.17(b) annual certification of compliance [verify]. The QA review over workpapers that touch the certification scope reads heavier on evidence reliability and conclusion support because the workpaper feeds the senior-officer certification posture. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Information Security booklet** [verify current edition and section]. Cyber control-testing documentation conventions and review expectations for federally-supervised banks. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/information-security.aspx [verify URL].

- **NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5 — Assessing Security and Privacy Controls in Information Systems and Organizations** (January 2022). Control-test documentation and review conventions, including assessment objectives, methods, and assessment objects [verify section labels]. Used as the methodology anchor for QA over cyber-control workpapers, especially the evidence-reliability dimension. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/r5/final .

## Cross-cutting conduct anchors (conduct overlay carries the detail)

- **CFPB UDAAP Examination Procedures** (CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual, UDAAP module) [verify section labels]. Used by QA to challenge legal-violation framing in conclusions on consumer-facing tests and to enforce the customer-harm-distinct-from-control-conclusion language. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/ .

- **Dodd-Frank §1031 (12 USC §5531) and §1036 (12 USC §5536)** — UDAAP authority. Statutory frame for "abusive" prong distinct from FTC Act §5 unfair or deceptive prongs; the QA reviewer challenges any workpaper conclusion that drifts toward statutory-violation language. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/5531 .
