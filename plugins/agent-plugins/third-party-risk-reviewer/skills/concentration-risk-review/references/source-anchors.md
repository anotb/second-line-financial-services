# Source anchors — concentration-risk-review

The named, dated regulatory and standard-setting sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

## Primary frame — third-party / ICT third-party concentration

- **Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management** (OCC Bulletin 2023-17 / FRB SR 23-4 / FDIC FIL-29-2023, issued June 6, 2023). Concentration is addressed in the planning and ongoing-monitoring sections; firms are expected to consider concentration risk in third-party relationships across the lifecycle [verify section numbering across the released bulletin]. OCC release: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2023/nr-ia-2023-53.html . FDIC release: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23047.html . FRB release: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20230606a.htm .

- **OCC Bulletin 2013-29 — Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management Guidance** (October 30, 2013). Predecessor framing on concentration in third-party relationships, still cited as supplemental in supervisory practice [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2013/bulletin-2013-29.html .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Outsourcing Technology Services booklet** [verify current edition and section structure]. Concentration considerations in technology service-provider use; risk-assessment, ongoing-monitoring, and contingency-planning sections carry concentration framing. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/ .

- **DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Article 29 preliminary assessment of ICT concentration risk** at the level of ICT third-party service providers and at the sub-contracting level [verify subarticle]. Article 28(2) proportionality. Article 28 general principles for management of ICT third-party risk; recital language on critical ICT third-party service providers and the EU oversight regime. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj .

- **EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (EBA/GL/2019/02)** — risk-assessment guideline addressing concentration risk including with the same provider, the same group, and the same geographical area [verify section number]. The outsourcing-register guideline (cross-referenced; the primary anchor for that artifact is `dora-register-builder`). https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/internal-governance/guidelines-on-outsourcing-arrangements .

- **Commission Delegated Regulation laying down the RTS on sub-contracting under DORA Article 30(5)** [verify Federal Register / Official Journal citation]. Elements the firm must assess before allowing sub-contracting in the chain that supports a critical or important function; the concentration view consumes the firm's assessment of these elements at the portfolio level. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ .

## Operational-resilience and interconnections frame

- **BCBS Principles for Operational Resilience** (March 2021). Principle on third-party dependencies; interconnections and interdependencies framing that anchors the technology-stack and sub-contractor-chain dimensions. https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d516.htm .

- **FSB papers on third-party dependencies in financial services and on cloud-services concentration** [verify exact paper title, section, and date]. Background framing for sector-wide concentration questions; cite where the question is sector-wide rather than firm-specific. https://www.fsb.org/ .

- **UK Prudential Regulation Authority Supervisory Statement SS2/21 — Outsourcing and third-party risk management** [verify section on concentration]. Cross-referenced as the UK overlay where the firm has a UK nexus; the substantive concentration framing aligns with the EBA outsourcing guideline. https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2021/march/outsourcing-and-third-party-risk-management-ss .

## US-bank-specific concentration anchors (banking overlay carries the detail)

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D (OCC Heightened Standards)** for $50B+ covered banks. §III risk-governance framework, §III.B risk appetite [verify subsection labels]. The risk-governance framework reads concentration as part of the risk-appetite cascade. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30 .

- **Bank Service Company Act, 12 USC §1867(c)**. Statutory examiner-access right; cited for the regulator's expectation that the firm can produce a portfolio-level view of bank service providers. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/1867 .

- **Joint statement on community-bank fintech due diligence** (OCC / FRB / FDIC, August 27, 2021 — third-party risk management: a guide for community banks) [verify exact title and date] [verify section]. Reads concentration on fintech-partner dependencies in the community-bank context. FIL-59-2021. https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2021/fil21059.html .

- **Joint Statement on Banks' Arrangements with Third Parties to Deliver Bank Deposit Products and Services** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, July 25, 2024) [verify exact title and date] [verify section]. Operative for sponsor-bank programs; the BaaS-partner-bank dimension reads against this statement.
