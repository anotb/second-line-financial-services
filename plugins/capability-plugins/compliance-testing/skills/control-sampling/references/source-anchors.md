# Source anchors — control-sampling

The named, dated regulatory and methodology sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

## Primary frame — sampling methodology and risk-response

- **AICPA AU-C 530, Audit Sampling** (Statements on Auditing Standards, AICPA Professional Standards, current edition). The conceptual frame: sampling risk, tolerable rate of deviation, expected rate of deviation, sample size determination, selection method, and projection of deviations. AU-C 530 §A14-§A17 on factors influencing sample size for tests of controls; §A18-§A22 on evaluating sample results [verify paragraph references against the current AICPA codification]. AU-C 530 is an external-audit standard; in second-line testing it is borrowed as a methodology anchor for control-test sampling, not as a direct legal requirement. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 530").

- **AICPA AU-C 330, Performing Audit Procedures in Response to Assessed Risks**. Frames the link from risk assessment to nature, timing, and extent of testing, including extent decisions that drive sample size [verify paragraph references]. Used for the rationale chain that ties sample design to assessed risk in the sampling memo. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 330").

- **AICPA AU-C 500, Audit Evidence**. Frames the sufficiency-and-appropriateness test for evidence the sample produces; cited as the methodology anchor for the limitations-and-reliance section of the sampling memo [verify paragraph references]. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 500").

- **IIA International Professional Practices Framework — Global Internal Audit Standards (effective January 9, 2025)**. Standard 13 (Planning Engagements) covers engagement-objective-driven scope and resource decisions, which include sampling design; the prior IPPF Standard 2310 (Identifying Information) and Standard 2320 (Analysis and Evaluation) carry forward into the new standard set [verify section labels under the current 2025 IIA standard set]. Used as the internal-audit methodology anchor for sampling rationale and reviewer-separation expectations. https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/ .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Audit booklet (April 2012)**, §IV (audit testing) and the appendix on audit-test programs [verify section labels against the current edition]. Examiner-facing detail on internal-audit and IT-audit testing scope, sampling, and population definitions used at federally-supervised banks. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/audit/ .

- **SR 08-8 — Compliance Risk Management Programs and Oversight at Large Banking Organizations with Complex Compliance Profiles** (Federal Reserve, October 16, 2008) [verify section labels for the testing-program scope, population coverage, and risk-based-scoping expectations]. Federal Reserve supervisory expectation that compliance-testing programs cover the right populations with risk-based scope. https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2008/sr0808.htm .

- **OCC PPM 5310-3 — Bank Supervision Process** [verify exact PPM number, section labels, and current effective date]. OCC examiner-facing supervision-process guidance referenced by OCC-supervised bank compliance and audit programs as the testing-program scope and population-coverage anchor [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html [verify URL].

- **CFPB Compliance Management System Review Procedures**, embedded in the **CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify section labels for the testing-program component]. Used for CMS-context expectations on monitoring-and-testing population coverage. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/ .

- **COSO 2013 Internal Control — Integrated Framework, Principle 16** (selects, develops, and performs ongoing and/or separate evaluations) [verify principle reference]. Used to situate sampling inside the monitoring component of the firm's internal-control framework. https://www.coso.org/internal-control .

## Payments-fintech-specific anchors (payments-fintech overlay carries the detail)

- **Joint Statement on Banks' Arrangements with Third Parties to Deliver Bank Deposit Products and Services** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, July 25, 2024) [verify exact title and date]. Operative for sponsor-bank programs; sampling memos for end-customer reconciliation, FBO subledger, and deposit-insurance representation testing sit inside this frame.

- **U.S. Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, June 2023) [verify exact title and Federal Register citation]. Ongoing-monitoring expectations drive sponsor-bank sampling cadence and population coverage.

- **Reg E — 12 CFR Part 1005**, especially §1005.6 (consumer liability), §1005.11 (procedures for resolving errors), and §1005.18 (prepaid accounts) [verify subsection labels]. Error-resolution case-file populations are the sampling target for Reg E compliance testing. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1005 .

- **Reg Z — 12 CFR Part 1026**, billing-error procedures (§1026.13 for open-end credit) [verify subsection labels]. Billing-error case-file populations parallel the Reg E error-resolution population. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1026 .

- **NACHA Operating Rules (current edition)** Third-Party Sender risk management and ODFI obligations [verify rule citations against the firm's licensed copy]. Paywalled; cite as "current edition; specific sections to be confirmed against the firm's licensed copy". https://www.nacha.org/ .
