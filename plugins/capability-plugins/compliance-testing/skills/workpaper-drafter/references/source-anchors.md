# Source anchors — workpaper-drafter

The named, dated regulatory and methodology sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

## Primary frame — testing methodology and documentation

- **AICPA AU-C 230, Audit Documentation** (Statements on Auditing Standards, AICPA Professional Standards, current edition). The documentation bar: documentation should be sufficient for an experienced auditor with no prior connection to the engagement to understand the nature, timing, extent, and results of audit procedures performed; the audit evidence obtained; the conclusions reached on significant matters; and significant judgments [verify paragraph references against current AICPA codification, generally AU-C 230 §08-§14]. Used by second-line testing programs as the documentation methodology anchor even where the engagement is not a financial-statement audit. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 230").

- **AICPA AU-C 500, Audit Evidence**. Frames the link between evidence and conclusion: the auditor designs procedures appropriate in the circumstances to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence [verify paragraph reference]. Used to justify the evidence inspected versus the procedure outcome versus the conclusion chain in the workpaper. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 500").

- **AICPA AU-C 530, Audit Sampling**, especially §A18-§A22 on evaluating sample results [verify paragraph references against current AICPA codification]. Frames the projection-from-sample-to-population read in the workpaper's aggregation section. The sample drives the conclusion on the tested population only when projection is appropriate; deviation rate observed versus tolerable rate is the operative comparison. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 530").

- **IIA International Professional Practices Framework — Standard 2330 (Documenting Information)** [verify section labels against the current Global Internal Audit Standards release; the IIA reissued the IPPF as the Global Internal Audit Standards effective January 9, 2025, and prior IPPF Standard 2330 maps to the new Standard's documentation requirements]. Documentation completeness, retention, and supervision-review expectations for internal audit and second-line testing programs operating under IIA standards. https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/ .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Audit booklet (April 2012)**, §IV (audit testing) and §V (audit communication) [verify section labels against the current edition]. Examiner-facing detail on internal audit and IT audit documentation expectations at federally-supervised banks; used as the bank-supervisory anchor for testing-program documentation. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/audit/ .

- **SR 08-8 — Compliance Risk Management Programs and Oversight at Large Banking Organizations with Complex Compliance Profiles** (Federal Reserve, October 16, 2008) [verify section labels for the testing-program documentation expectations]. Federal Reserve supervisory expectation that compliance-testing programs are documented to a standard supporting independent review. https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2008/sr0808.htm .

- **OCC PPM 5310-3 — Bank Supervision Process** [verify exact PPM number, section labels, and current effective date]. OCC examiner-facing supervision-process guidance referenced by OCC-supervised bank compliance and audit programs as the testing-documentation anchor [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html [verify URL].

- **COSO 2013 Internal Control — Integrated Framework, Principle 17** (committee evaluates and communicates internal-control deficiencies in a timely manner) [verify principle reference]. Used as the framework anchor for elevating exceptions to issues and reporting on testing results. https://www.coso.org/internal-control .

- **CFPB CMS Exam-Cycle Workpaper Conventions** (CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual, CMS examination procedures) [verify section reference]. Workpaper conventions consumer-compliance examiners apply when reading second-line testing files.

## Cross-cutting cyber anchors (cyber overlay carries the detail)

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500.7 — Access Privileges and Management**. §500.7(a) limit user access privileges to information systems that provide access to nonpublic information based on the user's role; periodic review of access privileges and removal of unnecessary access; require periodic certification by senior officer for risk-based authentication exemptions [verify exact subsection labels against the latest amendment]. The standard test scope for access-recertification workpapers at NYDFS Covered Entities. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500.17 — Notices to Superintendent**. §500.17(b) annual certification of compliance; relevant for whether workpaper findings touch the Covered Entity's annual certification posture. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Information Security booklet** [verify current edition and section]. Cyber control-testing documentation conventions for federally-supervised banks. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/information-security.aspx [verify URL].

- **NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5 — Assessing Security and Privacy Controls in Information Systems and Organizations** (January 2022). Control-test documentation conventions, including assessment objectives, methods, and assessment objects [verify section labels]. Used as the methodology anchor for cyber-control workpaper sample-level evidence treatment. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/r5/final .
