# Source anchors — test-plan-builder

The named, dated regulatory and methodology sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

`[verify section]` placeholders mark anchors where the precise section reference still needs verification against the current edition of the source. They are deliberate, not omissions.

## Primary frame — engagement planning and risk-response methodology

- **IIA International Professional Practices Framework — Global Internal Audit Standards (effective January 9, 2025)**. The standards covering engagement planning and the engagement work program: Standard 13 (Planning Engagements), Standard 9.1 (Internal Audit Charter), and the Standard requirements on engagement objectives, scope, criteria, and resources [verify section labels under the current 2025 IIA standard set; superseded the prior 2017 IPPF numbering of 2200, 2210, 2220, 2240]. Used by second-line testing programs as the methodology anchor on documenting objectives, scope, criteria, and procedures before fieldwork begins. https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/ .

- **AICPA AU-C 300, Planning an Audit** (Statements on Auditing Standards, AICPA Professional Standards, current edition) [verify exact paragraph references — generally §07 through §12 in the current edition cover the audit strategy and audit plan]. AU-C 300 is an external-audit standard; in second-line testing it is a methodology anchor on the planning artifact, the assignment of work, and revisiting scope as facts change. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 300").

- **AICPA AU-C 330, Performing Audit Procedures in Response to Assessed Risks** (current edition) [verify paragraph references]. Frames the linkage from assessed risk to the nature, timing, and extent of testing procedures. Used to anchor the design-and-operating procedure split and the rationale for procedure-level evidence expectations in the plan. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 330").

- **AICPA AU-C 530, Audit Sampling** (current edition), §A18-§A22 on evaluating sample results and §A02-§A09 on sample design considerations [verify paragraph references against current AICPA codification]. Used in the test plan as the methodology anchor for the population-and-sampling-reference section and for the tolerable deviation rate framing in the pass and fail criteria. https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-standards (search "AU-C 530").

## Bank-supervisory frame — compliance-testing-program planning

- **SR 08-8 — Compliance Risk Management Programs and Oversight at Large Banking Organizations with Complex Compliance Profiles** (Federal Reserve, October 16, 2008) [verify section labels for the testing-program scope and risk-based-prioritization expectations]. Federal Reserve supervisory expectation that compliance-testing programs apply a risk-based prioritization to the test plan and that the planning artifact supports independent review. https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2008/sr0808.htm .

- **OCC PPM 5310-3 — Bank Supervision Process** [verify exact PPM number, section labels, and current effective date]. OCC examiner-facing supervision-process guidance referenced by OCC-supervised bank compliance and audit programs as a planning-documentation anchor [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html [verify URL].

- **OCC Comptroller's Handbook — Compliance Management Systems** [verify current edition and section labels]. Examiner-facing handbook describing CMS testing-program planning expectations for OCC-supervised banks. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Audit booklet (April 2012)**, §III (audit planning) and Appendix A (audit work-program elements) [verify section labels against the current edition]. Used as the methodology anchor for the structure of the test plan and for the work-program elements (objective, criteria, walkthrough, procedures, evidence, sample, conclusion logic). https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/audit/ .

## Consumer-protection examination-program scoping

- **CFPB Compliance Management Review** examination procedures, embedded in the **CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify section labels for the testing-program component]. Standing examination procedures that read the firm's CMS, including the testing-program design and execution; used as the consumer-compliance scoping anchor for CFPB-supervised institutions. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/ .

- **FFIEC Consumer Compliance Rating System** (Federal Register, 80 Fed. Reg. 67428, November 2, 2015) [verify Federal Register citation and section]. Frames the consumer-compliance rating that examiner findings (often built on second-line testing) feed; the test plan's risk-based-prioritization section is read with this rating frame in mind for consumer-facing controls. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2015/11/02/2015-27738/ffiec-consumer-compliance-rating-system [verify URL].

## Heightened-standards frame — large-bank risk-governance posture

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D — OCC Heightened Standards for large insured national banks** (covered banks generally $50 billion in assets and above) [verify section labels and the current asset threshold]. Section III on independent-risk-management responsibilities and three-lines-of-defense; relevant to the test plan's risk-based-prioritization scope and to the reviewer-separation discipline. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30/appendix-Appendix%20D%20to%20Part%2030 .

## Framework anchor — monitoring and separate evaluations

- **COSO 2013 Internal Control — Integrated Framework, Principle 16 (selects, develops, and performs ongoing and/or separate evaluations)** [verify principle reference]. Used as the framework anchor for the test plan's role as a separate evaluation, distinct from ongoing monitoring activity. https://www.coso.org/internal-control .

- **COSO 2013 Internal Control — Integrated Framework, Principle 17 (evaluates and communicates internal control deficiencies)** [verify principle reference]. Used as the framework anchor for how exceptions surfaced under the plan are subsequently elevated by the testing program (the plan's downstream-consumers section names `exception-analysis` and `risk-compliance-core/skills/issue-writeup` for that lifecycle). https://www.coso.org/internal-control .
