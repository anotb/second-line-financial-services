The named, dated regulatory sources this skill anchors to for regulator-engagement convention. ## Federal banking supervision — engagement convention

- **Comptroller's Handbook — Bank Supervision Process** (OCC, current edition) [verify current edition and section]. The OCC's published supervisory process for national banks and federal savings associations: scope memorandum, first-day letter, on-site fieldwork, examiner-in-charge role, draft and final report, supervisory-letter and MRA-MRIA mechanics, exit conference. Public booklets at https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

- **Comptroller's Handbook — Internal Controls** (OCC, current edition) [verify current edition and section]. Examiner expectations for control documentation and evidence presentation. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

- **FDIC Risk Management Manual of Examination Policies** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. FDIC's examination process for state non-member banks, including scope-setting, examination-procedures sections by topic, and report-of-examination conventions. https://www.fdic.gov/regulations/safety/manual/ .

- **FRB Bank Holding Company Supervision Manual** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. FRB's published supervisory process for bank holding companies and savings and loan holding companies; consolidated-supervision framing. https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/supervision_manual.htm .

- **FRB Commercial Bank Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. FRB's published examination process for state-member banks. https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/cbem.pdf [verify URL].

- **FFIEC Examination Modernization Project** [verify section and current scope]. Inter-agency programme to modernise examination processes for community banks; evidence-handling and request-list conventions are evolving. https://www.ffiec.gov/ .

- **FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify section]. The inter-agency manual structure that drives BSA/AML examination request lists, scoping conventions, and examination procedures. The manual's structure is itself the request-list spine for any BSA/AML-focused exam. https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual .

## CFPB supervision — engagement convention

- **CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. The CFPB's published examination process for both bank and non-bank supervised institutions: scope, baseline-review-modules, examiner-in-charge role, supervisory-letter mechanics, Matters Requiring Attention. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/ .

- **CFPB Supervisory Highlights** (periodic) [verify section in most recent edition]. The public summary of recent examination findings; the firm's anticipated-questions list reads against the most recent edition. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/research-reports/supervisory-highlights/ .

- **CFPB Confidential Supervisory Information rule, 12 CFR Part 1070 Subpart D** (§§1070.40-1070.47) [verify subsection labels]. Defines CSI, restricts the firm's onward sharing, and sets the authorisation regime. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1070 .

## SEC and FINRA examination — engagement convention

- **SEC Division of Examinations annual examination priorities** (most recent letter) [verify section in most recent priorities letter]. Published annual statement of focus areas; the basis for anticipated-question patterns in adviser, broker-dealer, and investment-company exams. https://www.sec.gov/exams .

- **SEC Division of Examinations risk alerts** (periodic) [verify section in topic-specific alert]. Topic-specific alerts (marketing rule, custody, fee disclosure, MNPI, electronic communications) that drive request-list emphasis. https://www.sec.gov/exams .

- **FINRA Rule 8210 — Provision of Information and Testimony**. The FINRA rule under which member firms and associated persons are required to produce information and provide testimony in FINRA inquiries and examinations. Materially shapes document-handling and interview-prep posture for FINRA matters. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/8210 .

- **FINRA Examination Findings and Observations Report** (annual) [verify section in most recent annual report]. Public summary of recurring examination findings; the basis for anticipated-question patterns. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports .

- **FINRA Annual Regulatory Oversight Report** (annual). Combined examination priorities and findings; replaces the prior separate examination-priorities and examination-findings letters. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports .

## MRA, MRIA, and consent-order conventions

- **MRA / MRIA convention** — Matters Requiring Attention and Matters Requiring Immediate Attention are the federal banking agencies' two-tier supervisory finding mechanism. MRAs are deficiencies the bank must address; MRIAs are deficiencies of greater significance requiring immediate management attention. The convention is shared across OCC, FRB, and FDIC, with agency-specific procedural detail in each agency's published examination manual [verify agency-specific definitions and escalation paths]. The CFPB uses Matters Requiring Attention by parallel convention.

- **Public consent orders** — Use only published, finalised consent orders for structural pattern (response-letter shape, remediation-plan structure, monitor designation). Do not use named institutions in narrative beyond the public-defendant reference. Examples: published OCC, FRB, FDIC, CFPB, SEC, FINRA, NYDFS enforcement orders, indexed at each agency's public enforcement page.
