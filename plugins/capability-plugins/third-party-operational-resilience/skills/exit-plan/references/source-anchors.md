# Source anchors — exit-plan

The named, dated regulatory sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

## Primary frame — exit and termination expectations

- **Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management** (OCC Bulletin 2023-17 / FRB SR 23-4 / FDIC FIL-29-2023, issued June 6, 2023). Termination expectations: firms should plan for orderly transition or contingency on termination, including for unexpected termination, with consideration of customer-impact, data return and destruction, and contract obligations [verify section numbering across the released bulletin]. OCC release: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2023/nr-ia-2023-53.html . FDIC release: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23047.html . FRB release: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20230606a.htm .

- **OCC Bulletin 2013-29 — Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management Guidance** (October 30, 2013). Predecessor termination framing still cited as supplemental in supervisory practice [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2013/bulletin-2013-29.html .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Outsourcing Technology Services booklet** [verify current edition and section structure]. Contingency-planning and termination expectations for technology service providers, including data-conversion considerations and continuity of service. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/ .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Business Continuity Management booklet** [verify current edition and section structure]. Testing-and-exercise programme expectations: tabletop, walkthrough, simulation, full live exercise; recovery-strategy considerations including third-party arrangements. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/ .

- **FFIEC inter-agency cloud joint statement** (Security in a Cloud Computing Environment, April 30, 2020) [verify exact title and date]. Cloud-specific BCDR and exit considerations including data-portability and lock-in management.

- **DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Article 28(8)**. Financial entities must have exit strategies for ICT services supporting critical or important functions. The strategy must be comprehensive, documented, and sufficiently tested, with consideration of business-continuity risks, the time required for transition, and the protection of confidential, sensitive, and personal data [verify subarticle numbering against the official OJ text]. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj .

- **DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Article 30(3)**. Mandatory contractual provisions for ICT services supporting critical or important functions, including exit-strategy and transitional-support clauses, regulator-directed-termination posture, and supervisory-information rights [verify subarticle numbering]. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj .

- **DORA RTS on subcontracting under critical or important functions** (Commission Delegated Regulation [verify section, regulation number, and date of adoption / publication in the OJ]). Subcontractor-chain visibility and exit considerations on fourth-party arrangements. https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/direct-supervision-and-oversight/digital-operational-resilience-act/preparation-dora-application .

- **EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (EBA/GL/2019/02)**. §15 documented exit strategies [verify section number]. Includes scenarios for unexpected termination, transition-to-alternative-provider plans, and identification of indispensable functions. https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/internal-governance/guidelines-on-outsourcing-arrangements .

- **UK PRA SS2/21 — Outsourcing and third-party risk management** (March 2021) and **SS1/21 — Operational resilience: impact tolerances for important business services** (March 2021) [verify section]. UK overlay only. Exit posture sits inside operational-resilience programme; impact-tolerance framing is the test the exit plan must withstand. https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2021/march/outsourcing-and-third-party-risk-management-ss .

- **BCBS Principles for Operational Resilience** (March 2021). Substitutability and third-party-arrangement considerations for important business services. https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d516.htm .

## Bank-specific anchors (banking overlay carries the detail)

- **Bank Service Company Act, 12 USC §1867(c)**. Statutory examiner-access right over bank service providers. Examination access continues through wind-down; the contract clause and the actual evidence-preservation choreography are both required. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/1867 .

- **Reg DD — Truth in Savings, 12 CFR §1030.5**. Change-in-terms notification on deposit accounts. Drives the lead time for customer notice on a deposit-account-affecting transition. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1030 .

- **Reg E — Electronic Fund Transfers, 12 CFR §1005.8**. Change-in-terms notification on consumer-EFT-account services. Drives the lead time for customer notice on a transition affecting bill-pay, debit, or other EFT services. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1005 .

- **Joint Statement on Banks' Arrangements with Third Parties to Deliver Bank Deposit Products and Services** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, July 25, 2024) [verify exact title and date]. Operative for sponsor-bank programs; carries FBO-account wind-down, deposit-insurance representation review, and end-customer reconciliation expectations on exit.

## Insurance-specific anchors (insurance overlay carries the detail)

- **NAIC Insurance Holding Company System Regulatory Act (Model #440)** [verify model number] and **Holding Company Model Regulation (Model #450)** [verify model number]. Form B / Form D filings on affiliate-vendor termination.

- **NAIC Risk Management and Own Risk and Solvency Assessment Model Act (Model #505)** [verify model number]. Critical outsourced functions in the ORSA; transition events update the ORSA Summary Report.

- **NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law (Model #668), §4.F** third-party service-provider obligations including transition data-handling.

- **Policyholder protection statutes (state by state)**. State guaranty fund and policyholder-protection considerations on transition; portability of policy administration data; claims-handling continuity.

## Capital-markets-specific anchors (capital-markets overlay carries the detail)

- **Investment Company Act §15** — investment advisory contracts; shareholder vote and interim-contract authority on adviser or sub-adviser changes (Rule 15a-4 interim contracts). https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/80a-15 . Interim-contract rule: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-270/section-270.15a-4 .

- **Investment Company Act Rule 38a-1(a)(4)** — fund compliance-program rule; service-provider compliance-program-adequacy assessment for transfer agent, custodian, fund accountant, principal underwriter, valuation designee, pricing services. Service-provider transitions require fund-board notice and oversight. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-270/section-270.38a-1 .

- **Form N-CEN** — annual census report; carries certain service-provider-change disclosure items for registered funds [verify the specific items that capture service-provider changes]. https://www.sec.gov/files/formn-cen.pdf [verify URL].

- **Investment Advisers Act Rule 204-2 (books and records)**. Books-and-records continuity through a transition; the adviser remains responsible for retention regardless of vendor transition. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.204-2 .

- **17 CFR Part 248 (Reg S-P) as amended May 16, 2024** [verify exact compliance dates by entity type and size]. Customer-information-safeguarding obligations on transition; service-provider written-contract requirements including breach-notification; treatment of customer information on termination. https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2024/34-100155.pdf [verify URL].

- **Custody Rule, Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-2**. Qualified custodian transition mechanics; account-statement delivery continuity; surprise-audit posture across the cutover. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-2 .

- **17 CFR §240.17a-4 (broker-dealer books-and-records)**. Books-and-records preservation across a vendor transition. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/section-240.17a-4 .

- **Reg SCI — 17 CFR §242.1000 et seq.** Substitutability and BCDR posture for SCI entities; relevant where the vendor sits on an SCI system path [verify subsection].
