# Source anchors — vendor-diligence

The named, dated regulatory sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files.

## Primary frame — third-party / ICT third-party risk

- **Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management** (OCC Bulletin 2023-17 / FRB SR 23-4 / FDIC FIL-29-2023, issued June 6, 2023). Sets the risk-based lifecycle: planning, due diligence and third-party selection, contract negotiation, ongoing monitoring, termination [verify section numbering across the released bulletin]. Risk-based application; more detail expected for critical activities. OCC release: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2023/nr-ia-2023-53.html . FDIC release: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23047.html . FRB release: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20230606a.htm .

- **OCC Bulletin 2013-29 — Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management Guidance** (October 30, 2013). Predecessor framing still cited as supplemental in supervisory practice. Due-diligence and selection criteria, contract issues, ongoing monitoring [verify section]. https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2013/bulletin-2013-29.html .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Outsourcing Technology Services booklet** [verify current edition and section structure]. Risk assessment, due diligence, contract issues, ongoing monitoring of technology service providers. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/ .

- **DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Articles 28-30**. Article 28 general principles for management of ICT third-party risk; Article 28(2) requires firms to maintain ICT third-party risk management proportionate to size and risk profile [verify subarticle]. Article 29 preliminary assessment of ICT concentration risk. Article 30 key contractual provisions [verify subarticle structure of 30(2) and 30(3)]. Article 28(7) and 28(8) cover sub-outsourcing and exit posture [verify subarticle]. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj .

- **EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (EBA/GL/2019/02)**. Pre-outsourcing due diligence guideline [verify section number]; sub-outsourcing guideline [verify section]; outsourcing-register guideline (cross-referenced; the primary anchor for that artifact is `dora-register-builder`). https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/internal-governance/guidelines-on-outsourcing-arrangements .

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500.11 — Third-Party Service Provider Security Policy**. §500.11(a) policy elements; §500.11(b) periodic assessment. Identification, risk assessment, minimum cybersecurity practices, due diligence, periodic assessment. Applies where the firm is a NYDFS Covered Entity. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

## Bank-specific anchors (banking overlay carries the detail)

- **Bank Service Company Act, 12 USC §1867(c)**. Statutory examiner-access right over bank service providers; required contract flow-down (examination access, audit rights). Banking-only contract clause expectation. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/1867 .

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D (OCC Heightened Standards)**. Risk-governance framework for $50B+ covered banks; vendor-diligence outputs feed the framework and the board's third-party oversight. §III risk-governance framework, §III.B risk appetite [verify subsection labels]. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30 .

- **Joint Statement on Banks' Arrangements with Third Parties to Deliver Bank Deposit Products and Services** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, July 25, 2024) [verify exact title and date]. Operative for sponsor-bank programs; covers end-customer reconciliation, FBO subledger, deposit-insurance representation review, fourth-party register.

- **Joint statement on community-bank fintech due diligence** (OCC / FRB / FDIC, August 27, 2021 — third-party risk management: a guide for community banks) [verify exact title and date] [verify section]. Specific due-diligence framework for community banks engaging fintech partners; FIL-59-2021. https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2021/fil21059.html .

- **Comptroller's Handbook — Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management** [verify current edition and section]. OCC examiner-facing detail on diligence, contract, and monitoring expectations. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

## Insurance-specific anchors (insurance overlay carries the detail)

- **NAIC Bulletin on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems by Insurers** (December 4, 2023). Carrier accountability for vendor and third-party AI; AIS Program elements (governance, risk management, testing, validation, third-party-AI accountability, monitoring, documentation, transparency, fairness). State-by-state adoption; the NAIC AI Working Group adoption tracker is the public reference. https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/2023-12-04%20Model%20Bulletin_Adopted_0.pdf [verify URL].

- **NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law (Model #668), §4.F third-party service-provider obligations**. Due-diligence, written-contract terms, monitoring, incident-response cooperation. State-by-state adoption.

- **NAIC Insurance Holding Company System Regulatory Act (Model #440)** [verify model number] and **Holding Company Model Regulation (Model #450)** [verify model number]. Form B and Form D filings where the vendor is an affiliate of the carrier.

- **NAIC Risk Management and Own Risk and Solvency Assessment Model Act (Model #505)** [verify model number]. Critical outsourced functions belong in the ORSA Summary Report.

- **Colorado SB 21-169 implementing regulation** (3 CCR 702-10 §1 [verify section]) — governance and risk management of ECDIS and predictive models in life-insurance underwriting. https://doi.colorado.gov/ [verify exact citation].

- **NY DFS Insurance Circular Letter on AI and ECDIS in life-insurance underwriting** (January 2024) [verify exact circular number].
