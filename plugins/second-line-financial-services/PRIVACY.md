# Privacy

Second Line Financial Services is a static set of skill instructions, references, schemas, and templates. It does not run a service, collect or store personal data, send analytics, or make network requests of its own.

Some skills work on documents you give them, such as customer due diligence files, adverse-action notices, or complaint extracts, and those can contain personal data. Claude reads that material to do the work. The AI product you use processes it, and that product's privacy policy and settings apply. The plugin's author receives none of it.

Apply your firm's rules before sharing confidential supervisory information, suspicious-activity material, or personal data.

Ask questions through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/anotb/second-line-financial-services/issues).
