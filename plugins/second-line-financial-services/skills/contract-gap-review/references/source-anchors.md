# Source anchors — contract-gap-review

The named, dated regulatory sources this skill anchors to. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific and cross-cutting anchors live in the corresponding overlay files. `[verify section]` placeholders are deliberate and mark anchors where the precise section reference still needs verification against the current edition of the source.

## Primary frame — third-party / ICT third-party contract expectations

- **Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management** (OCC Bulletin 2023-17 / FRB SR 23-4 / FDIC FIL-29-2023, issued June 6, 2023). Contract-negotiation section enumerates expected clauses: scope and performance, responsibilities for compliance with applicable laws, audit and reporting rights, ownership and licences, business resumption and contingency, sub-contracting, confidentiality and information security, indemnification, limits on liability, insurance, dispute resolution, default and termination, regulatory access [verify section letter and subsection labels in the released bulletin]. OCC release: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2023/nr-ia-2023-53.html . FDIC release: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2023/pr23047.html . FRB release: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20230606a.htm .

- **OCC Bulletin 2013-29 — Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management Guidance** (October 30, 2013). Historical clause-expectation frame. The June 2023 interagency guidance is the operative anchor for new contract cycles. Where examiners reference 2013-29, treat as supplemental context, not the operative frame. https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2013/bulletin-2013-29.html .

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Outsourcing Technology Services booklet** [verify current edition and section structure]. Contract-issues section: SLA, performance reporting, problem reporting, regulatory examiner access, ownership and licensing, dispute resolution. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/ .

- **DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Article 30 (Key contractual provisions)**. Article 30(2) baseline contractual elements apply to all ICT-services contracts (function description, location and processing, availability/authenticity/integrity/confidentiality, data portability, SLD, incident assistance, competent-authority cooperation, termination, training). Article 30(3) enhanced contractual elements apply to ICT services supporting critical or important functions (quantitative/qualitative SLDs with performance targets, ICT security measures, TLPT participation under Article 26, unrestricted audit rights for the financial entity / appointed third party / competent authority, exit strategies). Article 30(4)-(5) cover sub-contracting and supplementary contractual content. Article 28(7) governs termination triggers; Article 28(8) governs documented and tested exit strategies. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj .

- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/532 of 24 March 2025** (OJ 2 July 2025, applicable from 22 July 2025) — RTS on sub-contracting of ICT services supporting critical or important functions under DORA. Sub-contracting clause expectations and conditions read against this. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/532/oj/eng .

- **EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (EBA/GL/2019/02)**. Written outsourcing-agreement section [verify section number]; rights of access, information, and audit; sub-outsourcing; termination; data location; cooperation with competent authorities. https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/internal-governance/guidelines-on-outsourcing-arrangements .

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500.11 — Third-Party Service Provider Security Policy**. §500.11(a) policy elements covering identification, risk assessment, minimum cybersecurity practices, due diligence, and contractual safeguards (multi-factor authentication, encryption, notification of cybersecurity events, access controls, representations and warranties); §500.11(b) periodic assessment. Companion clock at §500.17 cybersecurity-event notification (72 hours) [verify subsection labels under the most recent amendment]. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

- **FFIEC Joint Statement on Risk Management of Cloud Services** (April 2020) [verify section]. Shared-responsibility-model contractual expectations, configuration-management, operational visibility, audit, contractual remedies. https://www.ffiec.gov/press/pdf/FFIECCloudComputingStatement.pdf [verify URL].

## Banking-specific anchors (banking overlay carries the detail)

- **Bank Service Company Act, 12 USC §1867(c)**. Statutory examiner-access right over bank service providers; required contract flow-down (examination access, audit rights). Banking-only contract clause expectation; non-bank vendor frameworks do not include it. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/1867 .

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix B (Interagency Guidelines Establishing Standards for Safeguarding Customer Information)**. III.D service-provider arrangements: due-diligence, contract requirements, monitoring [verify subsection labels]. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30 .

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D (OCC Heightened Standards)**. Risk-governance framework for $50B+ covered banks; contract gap matrix outputs feed the framework and the board's third-party oversight [verify subsection labels]. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30 .

- **Joint Statement on Banks' Arrangements with Third Parties to Deliver Bank Deposit Products and Services** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, July 25, 2024) [verify exact title and date]. Operative for sponsor-bank programs; contract elements specific to BaaS and deposit-program operators (end-customer reconciliation, FBO subledger, deposit-insurance representation review, fourth-party register, BSA/AML responsibilities, customer-liability allocation).

## Insurance-specific anchors (insurance overlay carries the detail)

- **NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law (Model #668), §4.F third-party service-provider obligations**. Written-contract terms requiring information-security safeguards, monitoring, incident-response cooperation, breach-notification chain. State-by-state adoption.

- **NAIC Insurance Holding Company System Regulatory Act (Model #440)** [verify model number] and **Holding Company Model Regulation (Model #450)** [verify model number]. Form B and Form D filings where the vendor is an affiliate of the carrier; affiliate-services agreements have prescribed contract content.

- **NAIC Bulletin on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems by Insurers** (December 4, 2023). Carrier accountability for vendor and third-party AI; AIS Program elements binding on the carrier through the contract. State-by-state adoption tracker is the public reference. https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/2023-12-04%20Model%20Bulletin_Adopted_0.pdf [verify URL].

## Capital-markets-specific anchors (capital-markets overlay carries the detail)

- **Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-7** — adviser compliance-program rule; service-provider oversight requires contractual reach. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-7 .

- **Investment Company Act Rule 38a-1(a)(4)** — fund compliance-program rule, including service-provider compliance-program-adequacy assessment for transfer agent, custodian, fund accountant, principal underwriter, valuation designee, pricing services. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-270/section-270.38a-1 .

- **Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-2 (Custody Rule)**. Qualified-custodian status, account-statement delivery, surprise audit, SLOA inventory contract reach. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-2 .

- **17 CFR §§240.17a-3 and 240.17a-4 (BD books-and-records)** and **17 CFR §270.31a-2 (fund books-and-records)**. Retention and form requirements that translate into contract-clause expectations on records access, retention, and immutability. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17 .

- **17 CFR Part 248 (Reg S-P) as amended May 16, 2024** [verify exact compliance dates by entity type and size]. Service-provider written-contract requirements; safeguarding obligations; breach-notification timing. https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2024/34-100155.pdf [verify URL].

- **Reg SCI (17 CFR §§242.1000-1007)** for SCI entities. SCI policies-and-procedures reach into vendor arrangements supporting SCI systems; contract clauses on incident notification, BCP, change management. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-242 .

- **FINRA Rule 3110 (supervision)** and FINRA notices on outsourcing (Notice to Members 05-48 [verify]). https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/3110 .

## Payments-fintech-specific anchors (payments-fintech overlay carries the detail)

- **NACHA Operating Rules (current edition)** Originator and Third-Party Sender agreements, Third-Party Sender registration, ACH risk-management contract terms [verify rule citations]. Paywalled; cite as "current edition; specific sections to be confirmed against the firm's licensed copy." https://www.nacha.org/ .

- **Card-network operating rules (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)**. Required terms in issuer-processor and acquirer-merchant contracts; chargeback rules; PCI compliance representations; data-handling. Paywalled; cite as "current edition" and pin against the firm's licensed copy.

- **FinCEN MSB rules at 31 CFR Part 1022** and registration requirements at 31 CFR §1022.380 (initial registration within 180 days of becoming an MSB; renewal every two years). Third-party-relationship and agent-of-MSB arrangements have specific contract-content expectations. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1022 . FinCEN's MSB-registration page confirms §1022.380 as the operative section (https://www.fincen.gov/resources/money-services-business-msb-registration). Note: 31 CFR §1010.380 is the BOI / Corporate Transparency Act beneficial-ownership rule, a different obligation.

- **Reg E (12 CFR Part 1005)** §1005.6, §1005.11, §1005.18 — error-resolution and customer-liability allocation that the contract must support for the firm to meet its consumer-protection obligations. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1005 .

- **CFPB final rule defining larger participants of a market for general-use digital consumer payment applications** (December 2024) [verify Federal Register citation and current state including any litigation or congressional review]. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervisory-guidance/ [verify URL].

## Cross-cutting cyber anchors (cyber overlay carries the detail)

- **FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook — Information Security booklet** [verify current edition and section]. Third-party security-clause expectations; complementary-user-entity controls; incident-cooperation. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/ .

- **SEC final rule on cybersecurity disclosure** — Item 1.05 Form 8-K and Reg S-K Item 106 (effective 2023). Vendor-incident materiality determination and the four-business-day clock; the contract-side notification clock must align upstream with this disclosure window. https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2023/33-11216.pdf [verify URL].

- **NYDFS 23 NYCRR §500.17(a)** — 72-hour cybersecurity-event-notification clock for covered entities. Contract-side vendor-notification clock must close upstream. https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity .

- **CISA shared-responsibility model for cloud** [verify exact title and section of CISA publication referenced]. Contract clauses on responsibility split for IaaS, PaaS, SaaS tiers. https://www.cisa.gov/ .

## Cross-cutting privacy anchors (privacy overlay carries the detail)

- **GLBA Safeguards Rule, 16 CFR Part 314 as amended** (FTC, December 2021 amendments effective 2023). §314.4(f) service-provider contract requirements: select capable providers, contractually require safeguards, periodically assess. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314 .

- **GLBA Privacy Rule, 12 CFR Part 1016 (Regulation P)**. Joint-marketing and service-provider information-sharing exceptions and the contract terms required to use them. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1016 .

- **HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Parts 160, 164 Subpart E) and Security Rule (45 CFR Parts 160, 164 Subpart C)**. Business-associate-agreement content (45 CFR §164.504(e)) and breach-notification posture (60-day clock). https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/index.html .

- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by CPRA, Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq.** Service-provider and contractor contract clauses (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.140(ag) and §1798.140(j) [verify subsection]) and the prohibition on selling or sharing personal information. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?division=3.&part=4.&lawCode=CIV&title=1.81.5 [verify URL].

- **Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), Va. Code §59.1-575 et seq.** Processor-contract requirements (§59.1-579 [verify]). Companion in CTDPA, CPA, UCPA, TDPSA, and the rolling list of state comprehensive privacy laws. State-by-state pin to the firm's customer footprint.

- **EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689**. High-risk-system contractual obligations between provider and deployer; documentation, human-oversight, post-market-monitoring reach into the contract [verify article references]. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj .
