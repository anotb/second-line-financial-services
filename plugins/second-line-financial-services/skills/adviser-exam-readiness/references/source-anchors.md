## Investment Advisers Act of 1940 — rules

- **Rule 206(4)-7 — compliance program rule for investment advisers** (17 CFR §275.206(4)-7). Requires registered advisers to adopt and implement written policies and procedures reasonably designed to prevent violations of the Act and its rules, conduct an annual review of adequacy and effectiveness, and designate a Chief Compliance Officer. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-7.
- **Rule 204A-1 — code of ethics** (17 CFR §275.204A-1). Standards of conduct, reporting of personal securities transactions and access-person status, reporting of code violations, pre-clearance for IPOs and limited offerings. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.204A-1.
- **Rule 206(4)-2 — custody rule** (17 CFR §275.206(4)-2). Surprise verification by independent public accountant, account-statement-delivery test, qualified-custodian definition, SLOA / SPA exceptions, private-fund audit alternative. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-2.
- **Rule 206(4)-1 — Marketing Rule** (17 CFR §275.206(4)-1; adopted Dec 22, 2020; full compliance Nov 4, 2022). Single rule covering investment-adviser advertising and cash solicitation; replaces prior Rule 206(4)-1 advertising rule and Rule 206(4)-3 cash-solicitation rule. https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2020/ia-5653.pdf. Cross-skill linkage: `marketing-rule-evidence` carries the substantive evidence pack.
- **Rule 204-2 — books and records** (17 CFR §275.204-2). Full table of records required of registered advisers; retention 5 years (2 years on-site for some categories); electronic-records standards under §275.204-2(g). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.204-2.
- **Rule 204(b)-1 — Form PF** (17 CFR §275.204(b)-1). Reporting by certain SEC-registered private-fund advisers; thresholds for large hedge fund advisers, large liquidity fund advisers, and large private-equity advisers; current-event reporting under the 2023 Form PF amendments [verify the current effectiveness of the 2023 current-event reporting amendments post-PFA-Rule vacatur context — the Form PF amendments are a separate rulemaking from the vacated Private Fund Advisers Rule]. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.204(b)-1.
- **Rule 204-3 — brochure delivery** (17 CFR §275.204-3). Delivery of Form ADV Part 2A brochure to clients; annual offer; material-change interim delivery. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.204-3.

## Investment Company Act of 1940 — relevant rules for fund-adviser exam scope

- **Rule 38a-1 — fund compliance program rule** (17 CFR §270.38a-1). Fund-board adoption of compliance policies for the fund and its service providers; CCO appointment with board approval; annual written report to the board on operation of the fund's and service providers' compliance programs. - **Rule 17a-3 — broker-dealer records to be made** (17 CFR §240.17a-3). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/section-240.17a-3.

## SEC rulemaking — current state requiring verification at write time

- **SEC Cybersecurity Risk Management Rules for Advisers and Funds** — proposed Feb 9, 2022 (Release IA-5956 / IC-34497; proposed §275.206(4)-9 for advisers and §270.38a-2 for funds). Withdrawn from the SEC's regulatory agenda June 12, 2025 [verify exact withdrawal posture]. Not in force; not pending. The Reg S-P amendments are the operative customer-information safeguarding rule for advisers and funds. https://www.sec.gov/rules/proposed/2022/33-11028.pdf — historical record.
- **SEC Reg S-P amendments** (adopted May 16, 2024; Release 34-100155; amends 17 CFR Part 248). Customer-information safeguarding and incident-response notification for broker-dealers, investment advisers, investment companies, and transfer agents. Compliance dates: larger entities December 3, 2025; smaller entities June 3, 2026 [verify the larger-entity / smaller-entity scope cut against the operative final-rule definitions]. https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2024/34-100155.pdf.
- **SEC Private Fund Advisers Rule** (adopted Aug 23, 2023; Release IA-6383). Vacated by the 5th Circuit in *National Association of Private Fund Managers v. SEC*, June 5, 2024. Not in force; antifraud and recordkeeping reach via IA Act §206 and Rule 204-2 remains. https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2023/ia-6383.pdf — historical record. Vacatur opinion: 5th Circuit case 23-60471.
- **SEC Predictive Data Analytics Rule** — proposed July 26, 2023 ("Conflicts of Interest Associated with the Use of Predictive Data Analytics by Broker-Dealers and Investment Advisers"; Release 34-97990 / IA-6353). Withdrawn from the SEC's regulatory agenda June 12, 2025 [verify exact withdrawal posture]. Not in force; not pending. https://www.sec.gov/rules/proposed/2023/34-97990.pdf — historical record. `[verify section]`
- **SEC Names Rule amendments** (Rule 35d-1 under the ICA, amended Sept 20, 2023). 80% policy on names suggesting investment focus, including ESG-themed funds. **Compliance dates extended (SEC March 14, 2025): large fund groups ($1B+ net assets at most recent fiscal-year end) June 11, 2026; smaller fund groups (less than $1B net assets) December 11, 2026. - **Form PF 2023 amendments** (adopted May 3, 2023; current-event reporting) and 2024 amendments (adopted Feb 8, 2024; jointly with CFTC). The Form PF amendments are a separate rulemaking from the vacated PFA Rule and remain operative; verify current effective dates by adviser tier. https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2023/ia-6297.pdf.
