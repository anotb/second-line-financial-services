# Cross-cutting overlay — conduct

Loads when the exam scope includes best-interest, fiduciary-duty, suitability, or conflict-of-interest topics. EXAMS reads conduct evidence across nearly every IA / BD / dual-registrant exam; the overlay loads by default for retail-customer-serving firms.

## IA Act §206 fiduciary duty (15 USC §80b-6)

The IA fiduciary duty is the spine of conduct examination scope for advisers. The SEC Commission Interpretation Regarding Standard of Conduct for Investment Advisers (June 5, 2019; Release IA-5248) frames the duty as duty of care and duty of loyalty:

- **Duty of care** — duty to provide advice in the best interest of the client; duty to seek best execution of client transactions where the adviser has the responsibility to select broker-dealers; duty to provide advice and monitoring over the course of the relationship.
- **Duty of loyalty** — full and fair disclosure of all material conflicts of interest; not subordinating the client's interests to the adviser's.

The pack reads adviser conduct evidence against this frame:
- Investment-recommendation process (research, recommendation, suitability for the specific client, ongoing monitoring).
- Best-execution process (cross-skill linkage: `best-execution-surveillance`).
- Conflict inventory and disclosure (Form ADV Part 2A items 5, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14 disclosures and the supporting evidence).
- Soft-dollar arrangements under §28(e) safe harbour [verify current SEC interpretive posture].
- Principal trading and cross-trading approvals (Rule 206(3) procedures and Rule 17a-7 procedures for fund cross-trades).

## Reg BI (17 CFR §240.15l-1)

Broker-dealer best-interest standard for recommendations to retail customers. Four obligations:

- **Disclosure obligation** — written disclosure of all material facts relating to the scope and terms of the relationship and conflicts of interest at or before recommendation.
- **Care obligation** — exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill in the recommendation; understand the recommended product; have a reasonable basis to believe the recommendation is in the retail customer's best interest; consider reasonably-available alternatives for material recommendations.
- **Conflict-of-interest obligation** — written policies and procedures reasonably designed to identify and address conflicts including elimination of certain compensation conflicts (sales contests, sales quotas, bonuses, non-cash compensation tied to specific securities or product types within a limited time period).
- **Compliance obligation** — written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI.

The pack reads BD conduct evidence against the four obligations and reconciles to Form CRS disclosures, FINRA Rule 3110 supervision, and FINRA Rule 2111 suitability.

## Form CRS / Form ADV Part 3

Required relationship summary for advisers and BDs serving retail investors. The readiness pack confirms:
- CRS currency (last update date; material-change update evidence).
- CRS delivery evidence (initial delivery to retail customers; subsequent delivery on opening of new accounts; biannual update delivery if material changes).
- CRS website posting and consistency with Form ADV Part 2A (advisers) and BD disclosures.
- CRS consistency for dual-registrants (one CRS or two; the firm's election and the supporting rationale).

## FINRA Rule 2111 suitability

Operative alongside Reg BI for retail customers; primary standard for non-retail customers and recommendations of strategies. Three main components: reasonable-basis suitability, customer-specific suitability, quantitative suitability. The pack reads suitability evidence as a complement to Reg BI evidence — Reg BI is the primary standard for retail customers; suitability remains operative for institutional accounts and for the components Reg BI does not displace.

## Conflict-of-interest documentation

A recurring exam-deficiency theme. The pack carries a conflict register addressing:
- Proprietary-product recommendations.
- Affiliate revenue arrangements (revenue-sharing, sub-TA fees, 12b-1 fees, mutual-fund share-class selection).
- Principal trading and cross-trading.
- Soft-dollar / brokerage allocations.
- Compensation tied to specific products or product types.
- Outside business activities of supervised persons (Form ADV Item 11; FINRA Rule 3270 / 3280 for BDs).
- Personal-trading-related conflicts under Rule 204A-1 code of ethics.

For each conflict, the pack documents identification, mitigation control, residual conflict, disclosure mechanism (Form ADV / Form CRS / Reg BI disclosure / fund prospectus), and CCO sign-off.

## Mock-interview conduct themes

EXAMS interviewers probe conduct evidence through scenario questions. Common themes:
- "Walk me through how you decided to recommend product X to client type Y."
- "How do you reconcile the proprietary-product economics in your fee schedule with your fiduciary obligation?"
- "Tell me about the last time the firm declined a recommendation because reasonably-available alternatives looked better." (Reg BI / care.)
- "How do you supervise outside business activities of your representatives?" (BD / dual-registrant.)
- "How does the CCO learn about a code-of-ethics violation, and what gets escalated to the firm's senior officers?" (Rule 204A-1.)
- "What was the most material conflict you disclosed in the last Form ADV update, and what triggered the disclosure?"

The mock-interview prep section of the pack carries these themes calibrated to the firm's actual evidence; canned answers without evidence anchors fail the rehearsal.

## Pack carrying

The readiness pack reads conduct evidence across:
- Form ADV Parts 2A / 2B / 3 disclosure currency and consistency.
- Reg BI four-obligation evidence (BD / dual-registrant).
- IA Act §206 fiduciary-duty narrative (IA / dual-registrant) — investment process, best execution, conflict inventory, monitoring.
- Conflict register with control / disclosure cross-walk.
- Soft-dollar / §28(e) reliance evidence where applicable.
- Mock-interview prep keyed to conduct themes.
