# Fund board pack: [Trust / Fund Complex] — [Meeting date]

The deliverable is two artifacts the board materials secretariat distributes together:

1. **PowerPoint deck** — board-presentation slide pack the trustees read at the meeting; rendered via the `pptx` skill from the slide structure below.
2. **Word annex** — the deeper detail and the full §15(c) and Rule 38a-1 written content the trustees consult alongside the deck and that becomes part of the board minutes record; rendered via the `docx` skill from the annex structure below.

The deck is the primary read. The annex carries the §15(c) factor analysis at length, the Rule 38a-1 written report at length, the per-service-provider compliance program assessment, and the source trace.

The pack is a draft until the fund CCO and the board secretariat review and the independent trustees consume. The skill stops at the draft.

---

## PART A — PowerPoint deck (slide structure)

Each section heading below is one or more slides; the structure adapts to the meeting type (annual cycle vs. quarterly cycle vs. ad-hoc escalation).

### Slide 1: Cover

- Trust / fund complex name
- Funds in scope: [list, with fund type — open-end mutual fund / ETF / closed-end fund / BDC]
- Meeting: [Board of Trustees / Audit Committee / Compliance Committee] meeting, [date]
- Period covered: [start YYYY-MM-DD] to [end YYYY-MM-DD]
- Report type: [quarterly compliance section / annual Rule 38a-1 written report / §15(c) advisory contract renewal pack / ad-hoc escalation]
- Pack version: [v1 / v1-revised]

### Slide 2: Executive summary for trustees

Up to five bullets, in CCO voice. Read by the board chair before the meeting.

- [Headline matter the trustees should know about]
- [Material compliance matter count and any §38a-1 escalation]
- [§15(c) recommendation summary if this is a renewal cycle]
- [Service-provider matter requiring trustee attention]
- [Decisions or acknowledgements requested this cycle]

### Slide 3: Decisions and acknowledgements requested

The first thing the chair reads after the executive summary. A pack with no decisions or acknowledgements is unusual at a regular meeting; surface that absence as a sign-off question rather than ship a status pack.

| ID | Decision or acknowledgement | Recommended action | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | [decision or acknowledgement] | [vote-required / acknowledgement / noting] | [role — typically Independent Trustees / Audit Committee / Full Board] |

Full detail of each decision is in the annex.

### Slides 4-N: Rule 38a-1 written report (annual cycle) or quarterly compliance section (quarterly cycle)

- Operation of the fund's compliance program
- Operation of each service provider's compliance program: adviser, sub-advisers, transfer agent, custodian, fund accountant, distributor, principal underwriter, valuation designee, pricing services
- Material compliance matters since the prior report (count and type by service provider)
- Material changes to policies and procedures in the period
- CCO recommendation regarding adequacy (annual cycle)

Each service-provider line is a row on the slide; the per-service-provider detail lives in the annex.

### Slides for §15(c) advisory contract renewal pack (renewal cycle only)

One or two slides per Gartenberg / Jones v. Harris factor:

- Nature, extent, and quality of services
- Investment performance: period, benchmark, peer comparison; methodology pin
- Profitability of the adviser from the fund relationship; methodology pin
- Economies of scale and breakpoints; AUM growth analysis
- Fall-out benefits to the adviser and affiliates: soft-dollar credits, affiliated-broker revenue, 12b-1 flows, affiliated-bank deposit balances, sub-adviser economics, revenue-sharing
- Comparative fees and expenses: peer-group methodology pin; institutional-fee comparison per Jones v. Harris
- Trustee questions and adviser responses: substantive and period-specific

Full §15(c) factor narrative lives in the annex; the slides carry the headline conclusion and the trustee-question summary.

### Slide for liquidity (Rule 22e-4) — open-end funds in scope

- Classification distribution: highly liquid / moderately liquid / less liquid / illiquid
- HLIM compliance status (where applicable)
- 15% illiquid bucket status; any breach with restoration timeline
- Any board-approved program change in the period

### Slide for valuation oversight (Rule 2a-5)

- Valuation designee identification
- Valuation designee report summary for the period
- Fair-value events; overrides count and significance
- Prompt-notification log for the period
- Testing results summary

### Slide for derivatives risk (Rule 18f-4) — funds using derivatives

- Limited-derivatives-user status (or full DRMP)
- VaR-based limit type (relative VaR / absolute VaR) and current utilization
- Any VaR-limit breaches in the period; corrective action
- Stress-test summary; backtesting exceptions
- Annual derivatives risk manager report status (annual cycle)

### Slide for affiliated transactions

- Rule 17e-1 affiliated brokerage: aggregate commissions; quarterly procedure-adequacy determination status
- Rule 17a-7 affiliated cross trades: count and aggregate value; pricing methodology compliance
- §17(a) / Rule 17a-8 / §17(d) / Rule 17d-1 in-period activity
- Related-person disclosures and any change in §2(a)(19) interested-person status

### Slide for soft-dollar / §28(e) disclosure

- Commissions paid for research and brokerage in the period
- Eligibility analysis under §28(e)
- CSA / CCA arrangements; MiFID II treatment where applicable
- Any policy change in the period

### Slide for cyber and operational resilience events (when in scope)

- Material cyber events affecting the fund or fund shareholders in the period
- Service-provider cyber events meeting firm reporting threshold
- Reg S-P customer-notice posture for any in-period customer-information event
- Public-parent disclosure-referral status where applicable

### Slide for open issues and management actions

| Issue ID | Description | Severity | Owner | Due date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | [issue description] | [severity] | [role — typically the adviser CCO or service-provider CCO] | [date] | [open / in-remediation / closed-pending-validation] |

### Slide for forward-looking commentary

Named, dated, regulator-anchored items only. Generic forward-looking content fails the section.

- **Regulatory horizon**: [named upcoming or in-flight rule changes affecting the fund or its service providers, with implication]
- **SEC EXAMS focus**: [registered-fund focus areas in the current Examination Priorities relevant to the fund's product mix and rule reliance]
- **Industry enforcement themes**: [named themes the trustees should be aware of]

### Closing slide: Source trace and sign-off

- Source-trace appendix reference (full source trace lives in the annex)
- Confidence label: [high / medium / low / unknown]
- Sign-off block: fund CCO, board secretariat, independent trustees acknowledgement

## PART B — Word annex (section structure)

The annex carries the substantive detail the deck summarizes. The independent trustees and their counsel typically read the annex before the meeting and consult during.

### A1. Reporting period and meeting context

- Trust / fund complex name
- Funds in scope (with fund type and fiscal year end for each)
- Meeting type (regular / special) and date; prior-period meeting date
- Scope of report (drives required sections per the report type)
- Engagement-scope ID (if `scoping` was run): [ID]

### A2. Executive summary for trustees

Five-bullet narrative form (the slide is the headline; the annex carries the full sentence).

### A3. Rule 38a-1 written report (annual cycle)

#### A3.1 Operation of the fund's compliance program

Narrative covering the operation of the fund's compliance policies and procedures during the period; any material program changes; the CCO's assessment of adequacy.

#### A3.2 Operation of each service provider's compliance program

For each in-scope service provider (adviser, sub-advisers, transfer agent, custodian, fund accountant, distributor, principal underwriter, valuation designee, pricing services):

- **Provider**: [provider name and role]
- **Program adequacy assessment**: [parent CCO's reasonable belief, with the evidence basis]
- **Material exceptions in the period**: [list with severity and remediation status]
- **Oversight evidence**: [SOC 2 / Type II reports; independent attestation; testing conducted by parent CCO; exam findings; third-party reports]
- **Material program change in the period**: [list with adviser / parent CCO acknowledgement]

#### A3.3 Material compliance matters since the prior report

For each MCM in the period:

- **MCM ID**: [identifier]
- **Fund**: [fund name]
- **Description**: [what happened]
- **Root cause**: [analysis]
- **Remediation status**: [open / in-progress / completed]
- **Escalation level**: [reported / referred to enforcement / regulator-notified]
- **Owner**: [role]

#### A3.4 Material changes to policies and procedures

Annual cycle: list of material policy and procedure changes in the period across the fund and each service provider; CCO recommendation.

#### A3.5 CCO recommendation regarding adequacy

Annual cycle: the CCO's written recommendation that the fund's and each service provider's compliance programs are reasonably designed to prevent violation of the federal securities laws, with any qualifications.

### A4. §15(c) advisory contract renewal pack (renewal cycle only)

#### A4.1 Nature, extent, and quality of services

Narrative covering the adviser's services, the resources committed, the personnel allocation, the compliance and risk infrastructure, and any service change in the period.

#### A4.2 Investment performance

For each fund: performance against the fund's benchmark and against the peer group, for relevant time periods. Methodology pin: peer-group selection criteria, benchmark selection, period selection.

#### A4.3 Profitability

The adviser's profitability from the fund relationship. Methodology pin: direct expense allocation, indirect cost basis, capital usage. A bare profitability number without methodology fails the §36(b) discovery test.

#### A4.4 Economies of scale and breakpoints

AUM growth analysis since the last breakpoint adjustment; written analysis of why no further breakpoint is appropriate (where applicable); breakpoint structure as currently in place.

#### A4.5 Fall-out benefits to the adviser and affiliates

Substantive catalogue:

- Soft-dollar credits and §28(e) eligibility analysis
- Affiliated-broker revenue under Rule 17e-1
- 12b-1 distribution flows to the adviser's distributor affiliate
- Affiliated-bank deposit balances and cash-management revenue
- Sub-adviser-relationship economics where the sub-adviser is affiliated
- Revenue-sharing payments from the adviser to financial intermediaries

#### A4.6 Comparative fees and expenses

Peer-group methodology: how the peer group was selected; the peer-group composition; the comparison metrics. Per Jones v. Harris, the comparison addresses the adviser's institutional-account or sub-adviser-relationship fees against the fund-level fee for comparable services.

#### A4.7 Trustee questions and adviser responses

Substantive, period-specific questions from the independent trustees with the adviser's responses on the record.

### A5. Compliance and risk reporting cadence (every cycle)

Standing content the board reads every cycle. Some sections will be sparse in low-activity periods; the structure stays.

### A6. Liquidity risk management report (Rule 22e-4)

For in-scope open-end funds:

- Classification distribution (with timing)
- HLIM compliance and any change
- 15% illiquid bucket status; breaches and restorations
- Annual program-administrator report content (annual cycle)
- Stressed-market overlay where applicable

### A7. Valuation oversight (Rule 2a-5)

- Valuation designee identification and any change in the period
- Quarterly written report from the valuation designee
- Prompt-notification log for the period
- Fair-value override summary
- Testing results

### A8. Derivatives risk (Rule 18f-4)

For funds using derivatives:

- Limited-derivatives-user status and any transition in the period
- VaR-based limit type and current utilization
- VaR-limit breaches with corrective action
- Stress-test summary
- Backtesting exceptions
- Annual derivatives risk manager report (annual cycle)

### A9. Affiliated transactions

- Rule 17e-1 affiliated brokerage: aggregate commissions; quarterly procedure-adequacy determination on file; commentary on any procedure exception
- Rule 17a-7 affiliated cross trades: count and aggregate value; pricing methodology evidence against the §17a-7 conditions; annual board procedure review (annual cycle)
- §17(a) / Rule 17a-8 / §17(d) / Rule 17d-1 in-period activity with the §17 analysis basis
- Related-person disclosures and any change

### A10. Soft-dollar / §28(e) disclosure

- Commissions paid for research and brokerage in the period
- §28(e) eligibility analysis for the research and brokerage services received
- CSA / CCA arrangements
- MiFID II treatment for global advisers
- Any policy change in the period

### A11. Service-provider compliance summary

Standing per-service-provider summary table:

| [name] | [adviser / sub-adviser / TA / custodian / fund accountant / distributor / underwriter / valuation designee / pricing service] | [adequate / qualified / inadequate] | [list] | [SOC 2 date / attestation / testing] |

Cross-cutting cyber overlay content: material cyber events; service-provider cyber events meeting reporting threshold; Reg S-P customer-notice posture; public-parent disclosure-referral status.
