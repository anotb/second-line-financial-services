# Source anchors — implementation-plan

The named, dated regulatory sources this skill anchors to for implementation-plan structure, governance cadence, evidence-of-completion expectations, and remediation conventions. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Sector-specific anchors live in the corresponding overlay files in `references/sector-overlays.md`. Cross-cutting anchors live in `references/cross-cutting.md`.

## Federal banking — governance, remediation, and supervisory follow-up

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D — OCC Heightened Standards** [verify section]. The OCC's heightened standards for risk governance at large national banks: enterprise risk-governance framework, three-lines-of-defense, board oversight, escalation and corrective action. The framing for any material implementation programme at a Heightened-Standards bank. https://www.occ.gov/topics/supervision-and-examination/risk-management/heightened-standards/index-heightened-standards.html .

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix B — Interagency Information Security Guidelines** [verify section]. Information-security-programme expectations supporting GLBA Safeguards for OCC-supervised banks; remediation conventions for control-design failures. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30 .

- **OCC Bulletin 2014-24 — Heightened Standards Final Guidelines and FAQs** [verify section in current FAQ edition]. OCC's published expectations on the operation of the Heightened Standards framework, including remediation expectations and escalation paths. https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2014/bulletin-2014-24.html .

- **FRB SR 13-19 — Guidance on Managing Outsourcing Risk** (December 2013, Section II program elements). Third-party-touching elements of an implementation programme, including remediation-plan posture for third-party deficiencies. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1319.htm .

- **FRB SR 21-3 / OCC Bulletin 2021-X — Sound Practices to Strengthen Operational Resilience** [verify exact bulletin number]. Operational-resilience-touching elements of an implementation programme. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2103.htm .

- **Joint Interagency Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management** (OCC Bulletin 2026-13 / FRB SR 26-2 / FDIC FIL-15-2026, April 17 2026). One document issued through three agency channels with the same date, text, and signatories; supersedes and replaces SR 11-7 / OCC Bulletin 2011-12 (April 4, 2011) and SR 21-8 (April 9, 2021). The operative current standard for model-risk-touching elements of an implementation programme. The guidance is non-binding (Section I, footnote 1) and "expected to be most relevant to banking organizations with over $30 billion in total assets"; GenAI and agentic-AI models are explicitly out of scope (footnote 3). https://occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-13a.pdf ; https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm ; https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2026/agencies-revise-interagency-model-risk-management-guidance .

- **Superseded — SR 11-7 / OCC Bulletin 2011-12 (April 4, 2011) and SR 21-8 (April 9, 2021).** Cite as superseded when reading historical evidence (legacy validation reports, prior-cycle plans). Do not cite as the operative current standard. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1107.htm .

- **FRB SR 16-11 — Supervisory Expectations for Risk Management at Large Financial Institutions** (June 2016, Section II.A risk-management framework). Risk-management framing for material implementation programmes at large bank holding companies. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1611.htm .

- **FRB SR 95-51 — Rating the Adequacy of Risk Management Processes and Internal Controls at State Member Banks and Bank Holding Companies** [verify exact section]. Supervisory rating framework relevant to remediation programmes. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr9551.htm .

- **FRB SR 13-1 — Supplemental Policy Statement on the Internal Audit Function and Its Outsourcing** (January 2013, sections on internal audit's role in risk-management remediation). Internal-audit posture for implementation-programme review. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1301.htm .

- **MRA / MRIA convention** [verify agency-specific definitions]. Matters Requiring Attention and Matters Requiring Immediate Attention are the federal banking agencies' two-tier supervisory finding mechanism. MRAs are deficiencies the bank must address; MRIAs are deficiencies of greater significance requiring immediate management attention. The convention is shared across OCC, FRB, and FDIC, with agency-specific procedural detail in each agency's published examination manual. The CFPB uses Matters Requiring Attention by parallel convention. The MRA closure submission format and supervisory-letter response cadence drive implementation-plan milestone shape. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

- **FFIEC IT Examination Handbook — Management Booklet** [verify section in current edition]. Examiner expectations on project management, change management, and IT-programme governance. The booklet's management-control framework drives implementation-plan governance shape. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/management.aspx .

- **FFIEC IT Examination Handbook — Audit Booklet** [verify section]. Internal audit's role in change-management programme review. https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/it-booklets/audit.aspx .

- **Comptroller's Handbook — Internal Controls** (OCC, current edition) [verify current edition and section]. Examiner expectations for control documentation and evidence presentation; relevant to acceptance-criteria design. https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html .

## CFPB supervision — non-bank and bank consumer compliance

- **CFPB Supervision and Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. The CFPB's published examination process, including supervisory letter and Matters Requiring Attention conventions; remediation-plan expectations. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervision-examinations/ .

- **CFPB Supervisory Highlights** (periodic) [verify section in most recent edition]. Public summary of recent examination findings; the basis for trend-aware implementation planning. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/research-reports/supervisory-highlights/ .

- **CFPB Circulars** (issued periodically). Interpretive positions that drove design decisions in past implementation plans. **A broad set of CFPB Circulars (UDAAP, overdraft / NSF, adverse-action with complex algorithms, account reopening) was withdrawn by the Bureau on May 12, 2025; treat as historical only and anchor design decisions to the regulatory text rather than the withdrawn circulars.** The CFPB Interpretive Rule on BNPL (May 2024) was separately **nullified by Pub. L. 119-11 (May 9, 2025); historical only.** Reference any specific circular or interpretive rule as historical context where useful. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/circulars/ .

- **CFPB final rule, Small Business Lending Under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B), 12 CFR Part 1002 Subpart B Section 1071** (March 30 2023; **revised by the May 1, 2026 final rule**). Under the revised rule the threshold is a single 1,000-covered-transactions trigger; the rule is effective June 30, 2026 and the compliance date is January 1, 2028. The 2023 tiered schedule (Tier 1 / 2 / 3 at 2,500 / 500 / 100) is superseded. §1002.108 firewall; §1002.107 data points; §1002.109 reporting; §1002.114 effective dates. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/final-rules/small-business-lending-under-the-equal-credit-opportunity-act-regulation-b/ .

## SEC and FINRA — adviser, fund, and broker-dealer implementation

- **SEC Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-7** (17 CFR §275.206(4)-7). Compliance programme rule for investment advisers: written policies and procedures, chief compliance officer, annual review. The framework into which adviser-side implementation plans integrate. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-7 .

- **SEC Investment Company Act Rule 38a-1** (17 CFR §270.38a-1). Compliance programme rule for registered investment companies; fund board oversight of compliance programme. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-270/section-270.38a-1 .

- **SEC Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1** (17 CFR §275.206(4)-1) — Marketing Rule. Drives implementation plans for marketing-rule remediation. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-1 .

- **SEC Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-2** (17 CFR §275.206(4)-2) — Custody Rule. Drives custody-touching implementation plans. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206(4)-2 .

- **SEC Investment Advisers Act Rule 204-2** (17 CFR §275.204-2) — Books and Records. Drives books-and-records implementation plans, including the recent amendments to electronic recordkeeping. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.204-2 .

- **Securities Exchange Act Rules 17a-3 and 17a-4** (17 CFR §240.17a-3 and §240.17a-4). Books-and-records rules for broker-dealers. Rule 17a-4(f) addresses electronic records preservation. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240 .

- **SEC final rule, Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure** (Release Nos. 33-11216; 34-97989, July 26 2023). 8-K Item 1.05 and Reg S-K Item 106. Drives cyber-disclosure implementation plans for public companies. https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2023/33-11216.pdf .

- **SEC final rule, Climate-Related Disclosures** (Release Nos. 33-11275; 34-99678, March 6 2024). Reg S-K Item 1500 and Reg S-X Article 14. Status: the SEC voluntarily stayed compliance with the rule in April 2024 pending judicial review; the climate-disclosure implementation programme should not be sequenced against this rule unless and until it becomes operative again. State-level climate disclosure regimes (California SB 253 / SB 261) and EU CSRD remain operative for in-scope filers regardless. https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2024/33-11275.pdf .

- **CFPB final rule, Personal Financial Data Rights, 12 CFR Part 1033** (October 22 2024 final, Section 1033 of Dodd-Frank). **Status: compliance dates stayed as of October 29, 2025; CFPB-initiated reconsideration with ANPR.** The implementation programme should reflect the stay-and-reconsideration posture in effect at planning time and should not assume the original compliance dates. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/final-rules/required-rulemaking-on-personal-financial-data-rights/ .

- **SEC Division of Examinations annual examination priorities** (most recent letter) [verify section in most recent priorities letter]. The basis for anticipated regulator focus when planning implementation. https://www.sec.gov/exams .

- **FINRA Annual Regulatory Oversight Report** (annual) [verify section in most recent annual report]. Combined examination priorities and findings; frames anticipated regulator focus. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports .

- **FINRA Rule 3110 — Supervision**. Drives supervisory-procedure-touching implementation plans for FINRA member firms. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/3110 .
